## Eligibility

Must satisfy **all** of Terms art. 3:

1. Wanted member (join first if not).
2. **만 19세 이상** as of **참가 신청일**.
3. Team of **최대 5명**, every member a Wanted member.
4. **No duplicate participation** (1인 1회). After a registration is completed, no additional application (individual or team). (Landing FAQ)

Also:

- University / graduate / general public; employees, freelancers, sole proprietors OK if the age + membership tests pass. (Landing FAQ)
- Must not enter or submit in breach of an employment contract, third-party contract, or employer/school rules. Work must **not** be 직무발명 / 업무상저작물. (Landing FAQ; Terms art. 5.4)
- **원티드랩 및 계열사 임직원** may enter but are **excluded from awards and prize payment**. (Terms art. 6.6 — not stated on the landing FAQ)
- Membership withdrawal = lose eligibility and is treated as withdrawal from the contest. (Landing FAQ; Terms art. 10.4)
- Minors: UNIV20 writes “미성년자 참가 불가.” Official text is the age-19 test, not a separate “minor” clause.

## Team rules

- Representative / first registrant is **팀장** and files the application. (Landing)
- Invite link is issued **once** to the leader; usable only through **2026-09-18**. No adds after that date. (Landing FAQ)
- Team members must accept the invite (Wanted membership required).
- After an **individual** registration is complete, that person **cannot** later form a team. (Landing FAQ; 1인 1회)
- Unregistered collaborators may have developed the work, but if they hold rights, **written consent from all of them** is required in advance. Consent form: request `t-mktg@wantedlab.com`, submit by email **separately from** the task form. Invalid consent → eligibility limit or award cancel under Terms arts. **5 and 9**, and prizes already paid must be returned. (Landing FAQ)
- Leader represents the team for notices and withdrawal. (Terms art. 4.2–4.3)
- Partial member withdrawal: team continues if remaining members still qualify. **Leader withdrawal cancels the whole team.** (Landing FAQ; Terms art. 10.3)
- Affiliation (school/company) is hidden unless the participant opts to publish it. (Landing FAQ)

- **자유 주제.** Any project that uses AI to solve a real problem or create new value. (Landing)
- Existing / already-shipping / revenue-generating work is allowed if the participant or team **made it themselves**. (Landing FAQ)
- If already in service: separately email **운영 기간, 매출·수익화 여부** to `t-mktg@wantedlab.com`. Judges will use that as reference. (Landing FAQ)

- Host may use third-party **평가 솔루션** as reference; **final judging is by the judge panel**. (Terms art. 6.3)

## Withdrawal and notices

- In-product: **[내 과제] → [참가 취소]**. Cancel deletes draft/registered work with no restore; re-entry requires a new registration. (Landing FAQ)
- Withdrawal does not unwind already-run review or promotion. Award-stage withdrawal can cancel the award and require prize return. (Landing FAQ; Terms art. 10)
- TOP20 notice: Wanted-profile email **and** contest-site post. No reply in the stated window (Demo Day attendance, etc.) can drop the team from review/awards. Wrong email is the participant’s risk. (Landing FAQ; Terms art. 13)
- Team notice to the leader counts as notice to all members. (Terms art. 13.2)

## Prize payment (not scoring)

- Individual winner: participant account. Team winner: **팀장** account. Withholding tax may apply. Internal split is the team’s problem; host is done once it pays as announced. (Landing FAQ; Terms art. 7)
- Host may request 주민등록번호, account, and other legally required data. Unjustified refusal can block payment. (Landing FAQ; Terms art. 7.5)
- Host may substitute a benefit of **equal or greater** value for unavoidable reasons. (Landing FAQ; Terms art. 7.1)
